## Dialog

- **Dialog tags:** Prefer "said" and "asked." Avoid creative tags (exclaimed, queried, muttered, hissed) unless the word adds genuine information the reader can't get from the dialog itself.
- **Action beats vs. tags:** Prefer action beats ("He set down the glass. 'Fine.'") over dialog tags when the speaker needs identifying and there's a natural action available.
- **Capitalization after dialog tag:** "Go," he said. NOT "Go," He said.
